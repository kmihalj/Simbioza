# For survey authors

[Surveys](ankete_en.md)

This section is for people who write questions and review answers. You do not need programming knowledge or access to external sign-in configuration.

## Your first survey

1.  [Create a draft](ankete-izrada-objava_en.md) — choose a title and primary language.
2.  [Add an introduction and questions](ankete-pitanja_en.md) — explain the purpose and enter choices.
3.  [Choose who can participate](ankete-pristup-privatnost_en.md) — access and anonymity.
4.  [Check and submit for publication](ankete-objava_en.md) — only a published survey accepts answers.
5.  [View the results](ankete-rezultati_en.md) — charts, submissions and notifications.

**Example used in this guide:** “Your workshop experience” has a short introduction, a choice question, a 1–5 rating and an optional comment.

## When you need more

[Conditional questions and languages](ankete-grananje-prijevodi_en.md) · [Copying, closing, archiving and removal](ankete-kopiranje-backup_en.md)

If you cannot create a survey, ask your Workspace manager or administrator for the required permissions. Being signed in does not automatically give authoring rights.
