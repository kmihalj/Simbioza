# Working together on content

Simbioza connects shared pages with discussion, task lists and notifications. Choose the action you need; you do not have to be an administrator.

| Goal | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Write a comment or react to one | [Comments](komentari_en.md) |
| Add tasks to a page or mark work complete | [Tasks](zadaci_en.md) |
| Follow changes and choose email or in-app delivery | [Following and notifications](pracenje-obavijesti_en.md) |
| Find published content | [Search](pretraga_en.md) |
| Sign in or change personal settings | [My profile](osobni-profil_en.md) |

Features depend on enabled modules and your permissions. Reading a page does not automatically allow editing its text, and following content does not grant access to private information.

**Example:** an author publishes a workshop plan, a reader posts a question, an authorised person marks preparation complete, and followers receive the change through their selected channel. Saving an unpublished draft is not a new publication for followers.
